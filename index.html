About CUI Wah Hub

CUI Wah Hub is a dedicated, personalized tool built specifically for navigating the rigorous academic grading criteria at CUI Wah. Unlike generic calculators, this platform uses the exact official grading scales and weightage splits (such as the 67% theory and 33% lab distribution).

Designed as a single-user companion app, it securely stores your course data in your local browser, allowing you to track your cumulative degree progress semester by semester. It is your personal academic assistant to plan, predict, and achieve your target grades.

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers to common questions about your grade calculator.

Is the GPA calculator based on COMSATS criteria?
Yes, both the Course Calculator and the Degree Dashboard use the exact official COMSATS grading policy (e.g., A = 85+ / 4.00, C = 60+ / 2.00).
Where is my data saved?
Currently, all your courses and dashboard metrics are saved securely in your browser's local storage. This means your data is private to your device.
Can I export my dashboard history?
Yes! Go to the Dashboard page and use the "Export Backup" button to save your entire degree history as a file.
